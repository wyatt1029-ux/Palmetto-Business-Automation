# PBA search visibility and backlink routine

This is a review checklist, not an automated link-building campaign. Search rankings and backlinks cannot be guaranteed.

## Monthly check (about 30 minutes)

1. In Google Search Console, review Search performance, Page indexing, and **Links → External links**. Record the pages receiving impressions and the linking sites. The Links report is a sample, not a complete backlink inventory.
2. Open any new or unfamiliar referring pages. Keep relevant editorial, client-approved, and local-organization mentions; do not chase links from unrelated directories or link networks.
3. Check that the linked PBA page still serves the information promised by the referring page. Fix broken destinations with a redirect, not by dropping the link.
4. Review the public service area, phone, site URL, and business name for consistency across the PBA site and any business profiles. Do not publish a private street address.
5. Identify one real customer question or completed project insight that could become a useful page update. Obtain client permission before naming a client or showing their work.

## Good link opportunities to review, not submit automatically

- A verified Google Business Profile for PBA as a service-area business, with the address hidden if customers are not served there.
- Local chamber/member profiles **if PBA is actually a member**, such as the Charleston Metro Chamber or Berkeley Chamber. Check membership costs and profile rules first.
- A partner or client case study only when the other business independently agrees to publish or link to it.
- A practical guide, such as `/website-intake-checklist/`, shared when it genuinely answers a local business group's question.

Do not buy links for ranking, join link exchanges, auto-submit to directories, place keyword-stuffed footer links on client sites, or publish invented testimonials and results. Google treats these patterns as link spam.
