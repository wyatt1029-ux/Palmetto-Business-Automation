How working with PBA works

A clear engagement from first request to optional care.

  1. 01

    Start With Your Request

    Tell us what is slowing the business down, what you want to improve, or what you need built.

  2. 02

    Define the Need

    PBA documents the goal, recommended approach, deliverables, assumptions, and exclusions.

  3. 03

    Review & Approve the Scope

    You receive a written scope and price. No project work begins until you approve it.

  4. 04

    Secure Payment & Kickoff

    For one-time projects, payment is due at approval. Once approval and payment are complete, PBA schedules kickoff.

  5. 05

    Build, Review & Deliver

    PBA builds the agreed solution, shares progress as appropriate, and delivers it for review.

  6. 06

    Handoff & Optional Care

    You receive the completed work and relevant access or documentation. Ongoing care is available when it is useful.

Projects are scoped and priced clearly before work begins. Work outside the agreed scope is quoted and approved separately.
